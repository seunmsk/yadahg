Questions

Before you ask

Does it cost more to buy through a broker?

No. Insurers pay brokers a commission out of the premium, so you pay the same, and often less, because we compare several insurers before you commit.

Who do I pay my premium to?

You pay the insurer directly into its account, and you receive the policy document in the insurer's name. We never ask you to pay a premium into a personal account.

Is third-party motor insurance enough?

It's the legal minimum, and it pays for damage you cause to other people and their property. It does not repair or replace your own car. Comprehensive cover does.

My policy is with another broker or insurer. Can you still help?

Yes. Send us the schedule before renewal and we'll check whether you're properly covered and fairly priced. You can move your business to us at renewal.

How fast are claims paid?

It depends on the insurer and how complete the documents are. We collect what's needed, submit it, and follow up until the claim is settled, and you can see each stage with your claim reference.
