The current site

Where yadahg.com lets the firm down

Everything below can be checked on the live site today. Each card says what is wrong, why it matters and how the new site handles it.

Security

Browsers mark the site "Not secure"

yadahg.com has no working SSL certificate. Chrome shows a red "Not secure" label and crosses out the https, and Safari shows a warning symbol. For a firm that handles policy details and claims, that warning sends people away, and Google ranks insecure sites lower.

a free SSL certificate from Cloudflare, renewed automatically.
Missed business

No way to get a quote online

A visitor who wants motor or group life cover can't get a price, send their details or book a call. The only option is a chat widget.

a one-minute quote request on the first screen, sent straight to the team.
Missed business

Nothing for claims or renewals

Claims are where a broker proves its value, but the site has no way to report one, send photos or check progress, and nothing to bring clients back at renewal.

online claim reporting with photo upload, claim tracking by reference, and renewal reminders.
Credibility

Spelling errors on the homepage

Visible mistakes make a regulated financial firm look careless.

"bonified member" · "bespoken services" · "Life in very uncertain" · "our loves ones are well are protected" · "reassurance services" (for reinsurance) every line rewritten and proofread.
Credibility

The firm's strengths are buried

The NAICOM licence, NCRIB membership and 20+ years in business sit inside a long paragraph, and the President/CEO's photo sits next to a general explanation of insurance. Contact details only appear at the very bottom of the page, and there is no menu.

licence badges in the first screen, the CEO, board and management in About, a clear menu, and phone, WhatsApp and email on every page.
Credibility

Textbook copy instead of a reason to choose Yadah-G

Much of the page explains what insurance is ("Why insurance?", a definition of insurance coverage). It never says why a client should use a broker, or this broker.

a clear promise: we compare the insurers, place the cover and handle the claim, at no extra cost to you.
Missed business

Corporate clients get little to act on

Group life and industrial all risk are the firm's core corporate covers, but there's nothing that invites a company to start a conversation.

a corporate section covering six business lines, with a free risk review booking form.
The new site

What I have built

Yadah-G's own details and logo, rebuilt around what clients actually come to do: get a price, buy cover, claim and renew.

Homepage

A quote request in under a minute

The first screen has rotating photos of the risks Yadah-G covers and says what a broker does for the client. Visitors pick a cover, leave their name and phone number, and the request lands with the team. No prices are shown, since insurers' rates change often.

Covers

Personal and business, clearly laid out

  • Motor, home, health and life for individuals and families
  • Group life, industrial all risk, fire, goods in transit and professional indemnity for companies
  • Reinsurance placement for insurers
  • A photo for every cover, set in Lagos where it fits
  • Every cover links straight to a quote request
Claims

Report it, then follow it to payment

Clients report a claim with photos or a police report and get a reference number. With that reference and their phone number, they can check each stage, from received to settled, with notes from the team.

Renewals

Clients come back every year

Anyone can set a renewal reminder, even for a policy bought elsewhere. The countdown shows how close they are to lapsing, and the team gets a list sorted by expiry date. Each renewal is a chance to win business from other brokers.

Staff desk

The team runs it themselves

  • Summary of new quotes, open claims, renewals due and calls to return
  • Update a claim's stage. The client sees it when tracking
  • Renewals sorted by expiry, with a WhatsApp reminder button
  • Every request saved, with its status and history
  • Optional email alerts for every new request
Mobile

Built for the phone first

Most visitors in Lagos arrive on a phone, often on mobile data. The new site is one light page that loads fast, with WhatsApp a thumb away on every screen.
